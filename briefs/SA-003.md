# SA-003 — Stage health refill and arcade sound effects

Producer status: SPECIFIED. Revision of QA READY SA-002. Project: D:/Downloads/TRABALHO/BLUESPOT DS/Space Attack G2i.

## Goal, scope and assumptions
Confirmed request: refresh player life on passing a stage; add simple audio for player and enemy. Preserve existing graphics and formation/dive gameplay.
Assumptions: life means the existing hull health, fully restored to100 on legitimate stage advancement. Simple audio means short, distinct procedural effects for player firing, actual player damage, enemy firing and enemy destruction. Add a compact accessible sound on/off button (and optional documented M shortcut); default sound enabled after a user gesture. No music, audio downloads, network dependencies or persistent preferences required.

## Behavior
1. At the existing transition after all enemies are destroyed and the inter-stage delay completes, restore health to100 and immediately update percentage, health-bar width and healthy color while incrementing wave. Preserve score, difficulty progression and normal next-wave formation. Do not refill during surviving-diver return, pause/resume, ordinary frame updates or game over. A player killed during inter-stage delay remains GAME OVER and cannot be revived by pending advancement. Start/restart already restores100 and remains consistent. Avoid unintended new immunity or scoring changes.
2. Play one short player-fire sound only when a player projectile is actually created (existing0.15-second cadence), one hit sound only when damage() really subtracts health, one enemy-fire sound per emitted enemy projectile and one destruction sound when an enemy is actually removed by a hit/contact. Existing contact scoring remains unchanged. Invulnerable rejected hits and mere survivor returns must not generate damage/destruction sounds.
3. Use a single lazily created WebAudio AudioContext with short oscillator/noise tones and conservative gain; effects are audibly distinct. Unlock/resume from actual start/resume/restart/button or keyboard user gestures rather than autoplay on page load. Handle unsupported APIs, suspended context or rejected resume without exceptions/unhandled rejections or blocking gameplay. Avoid accumulating AudioContexts, nodes, timers or event handlers; stop/disconnect voices and bound overlapping effects.
4. Sound toggle exposes clear label and accessible state, silences active voices promptly, prevents new audible voices while muted, and preserves preference through pause/restart. Unmute after a gesture makes subsequent effects work. Pause/focus loss and game over stop ongoing sound; no gameplay events or sound emission while paused/over. The lethal damage effect may briefly complete only if intentionally documented; otherwise use prompt silence on game over. No UI control should cause Space/Enter to accidentally start duplicate missions.
5. Update README with full health restoration, effects and mute control. Preserve layout and controls at1440x900 and800x600; styles may receive only the minor sound-button addition needed.
